## WHAT?
- The dashboard provides an interactive view of how Adventure Works Cycle is performing across its two sales channels: Internet and Reseller. It is built to showcase high and under-performance areas, analyze channels and effectiveness of promotions, and to be able to make faster, evidence-based decisions regards pricing, production, and marketing focus.
---
## HOW?
- To Enter
1. Open the dashboard link above in any web browser (desktop, tablet, and mobile are applicable)
2. Sign in with Microsoft Account
3. The dashboard opens on the **Home** page by default

- Navigation

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Starts here by default. An introduction of the team. |
| **Executive Overview** | The summary. Showcase company-wide revenue, profit, and the key Internet vs. Reseller channel comparison. |
| **Customer Insights** | Who is buying (Internet Sales customers only) - demographics, income, occupation. |
| **Product Performance** | Which products and categories are driving revenue and profit, by quarter and by region. |
| **Marketing Performance** | How discounts and promotions are being used, and whether they're working. |

- **Click any slicer** (the dropdown button, usually top-left of a page) to filter the whole page — e.g., clicking "Europe" under Territory will filter every chart on that page to Europe only. Click it again to deselect and return to viewing all data.
- **Click a bar, bubble, or map dot** on most charts to highlight related data across the rest of the page.
- **Hover over any chart** to see exact figures in a tooltip.
- **Drill-through** on the executive page, head over to the slicer titled "Sales Territory Group", hover on a continent, and right click, and look for a "Drill through" option to jump to the Product Performance page, a more detailed page filtered to what you clicked.

- Interpretation
**Executive Overview**
- The three cards (Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Profit Margin %) are your company-wide health check.
- The **Internet vs. Reseller comparison table**: it shows that while Reseller Sales generates the majority of revenue, Internet Sales is dramatically more profitable per dollar. This single finding should inform any conversation about where to focus growth investment.
- The revenue trend chart and map give context on timing and geography.
**Customer Insights**
- All charts here reflect **Internet Sales customers only** — Reseller partners are businesses, not individuals, so demographic data doesn't apply to that channel.
- Use this page to identify which customer profiles (occupation, income bracket, lifestyle indicators) are most valuable, for targeted marketing or bundling decisions.
**Product Performance**
- The quarterly revenue chart and Top/Bottom product charts identify what's working and what isn't at the SKU level.
- The **Revenue vs. Profit Margin scatter chart**: it shows Bikes drive most revenue but have low margin, while Accessories have the opposite profile. This supports a recommendation to shift marketing/production emphasis toward higher-margin categories.
- Use the Territory slicer on this page to see how performance varies by region.
**Marketing Performance**
- This page answers whether promotional discounts are working. The headline finding: 96% of orders happen at full price, and order size doesn't change when a discount is applied — meaning discounts are not currently driving additional volume.
- Discounts are also concentrated on Bikes specifically, the company's lowest-margin category — worth revisiting as a pricing/promotion strategy question.
---
## SCOPE AND LIMITATIONS
- Data covers **December 2010 through early 2014**
- Reseller Sales data ends approximately two months before Internet Sales data; charts comparing both channels are filtered to periods where both have complete data, to avoid misleading partial-period comparisons.
- This dashboard uses Microsoft's AdventureWorksDW2025 sample dataset as required coursework; all figures reflect that dataset and not live company data.
